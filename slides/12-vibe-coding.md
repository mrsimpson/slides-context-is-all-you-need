**Reader Notes:**

In February 2025, Andrej Karpathy (former Tesla AI director, OpenAI founding member) coined "vibe coding" to capture the experimental, almost magical experience of working with rapidly improving LLMs.

Twelve months later (February 2026), he declared the term obsolete. The capability leap was so dramatic that what was playful exploration became "default workflow for professionals." He proposed "Agentic Engineering" to emphasize:
- You're orchestrating agents, not writing code directly
- There's professional expertise and depth to develop
- It's engineering, not just experimentation

**But naming the discipline is just the start.**

Karpathy acknowledges there's "art & science" to agentic engineering - "something you can learn and become better at, with its own depth of a different kind." That means we need methodologies.

**Context Engineering is a methodology within Agentic Engineering.**

When you're orchestrating AI agents as a professional discipline, you need systematic approaches for:
- Providing the right context at the right time
- Managing information across different development phases
- Ensuring quality without constant human intervention
- Scaling collaboration as autonomy increases

The shift from "vibe coding" to "agentic engineering" happened in one year because of rapid capability growth. But transitioning from experimentation to professional practice requires more than a name change - it requires methodologies for actually doing it well.

That's what we're exploring: a systematic approach to context provision as a core methodology within the broader discipline of agentic engineering.
