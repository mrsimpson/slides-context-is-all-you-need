# LLMs have matured in three dimensions

**Intelligence:** Graduate-level reasoning (39% → 90%)  
**Memory:** Context capacity (4K → 1M tokens)  
**Autonomy:** Task completion (10 min → 10 hours)

**Reader Notes:**

In the beginning I spoke about my daughter and the approach to building Lego.

Here's what I don't tell my daughter about this programming-as-parenting parallel - she's not quite ready for yielding predictable results without specific instructions yet.

But LLMs? They've reached that level of maturity, and we can prove it. Just like child development experts measure cognitive ability, working memory, and independent functioning, we can now measure LLM maturity across three parallel dimensions:

**Intelligence (Graduate-Level Reasoning):**  
In just two years, LLMs went from 39% accuracy on graduate-level questions to approaching 90% - matching and sometimes exceeding human expert performance. These aren't questions you can Google - they're specifically designed to be "Google-proof," the kind that stump PhD students even with 30 minutes of internet research.

**Memory (Context Capacity):**  
Context windows grew 250-fold, from 4,000 tokens (a few pages) to over 1 million tokens (multiple full novels). An LLM can now "hold in mind" your entire codebase, all your documentation, and the full conversation history simultaneously.

**Autonomy (Task Completion):**  
The length of tasks LLMs can complete independently has been doubling every 4-7 months. What required 10 minutes of expert time in early 2024 now takes 10 hours - and at this accelerating pace, we're looking at day-long tasks within a year.

When all three dimensions mature together, something qualitative emerges. Just like children reach a point where they can handle complex tasks independently when given context and boundaries, LLMs have crossed that same threshold.

The opportunity is enormous - we can move from micromanaging every step to providing the right context and letting them work intelligently within those boundaries. And the numbers prove this isn't hype - it's a measurable, reproducible reality.
